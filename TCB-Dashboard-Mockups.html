Conversion rates iEach rate is a place a lever can move. Booked to taken is the primary focus.

The chain of rates that turns spend into cash.

Read me first: of every 10 paid calls booked, only about 3 actually happen. Lifting booked to taken from 29% to 45% is roughly 9 more closes a month at no extra ad spend. That is the single biggest lever, which is why the Appointments page is the one to watch.
